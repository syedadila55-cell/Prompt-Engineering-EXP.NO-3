Exploring the Different Types of Prompts
1. Introduction
Artificial Intelligence systems like ChatGPT depend on prompts to generate responses. A
prompt is the input given by a user to guide the AI in producing meaningful output. The
effectiveness of AI responses depends largely on how well the prompt is structured.
Prompting is considered an important skill in modern computing because it helps users
communicate clearly with AI systems.
🎯 2. Objectives of the Experiment
To understand the concept of prompting
To explore different types of prompts
To learn how prompt structure affects output
To analyze examples of different prompting techniques
To apply prompting techniques effectively
🧠 3. Definition of Prompt
A prompt is a statement, instruction, or question given to an AI system to perform a task or
generate a response.
Example:
“Explain global warming”
“Create a table of fruits and their benefits”

✏️ 5. Straightforward Prompts
🔹 5.1 Definition
A Straightforward Prompt is a simple and direct instruction or question given to an AI without
any additional structure or complexity.
🔹 5.2 Features
Direct and clear
Easy to understand
No formatting instructions
Quick responses
🔹 5.3 Examples
“What is photosynthesis?”
“Define democracy.”
“List the types of energy.”
🔹 5.4 Advantages
Easy to use
Saves time
Suitable for beginners
Quick responses
🔹 5.5 Disadvantages
Limited detail
Less control over output
May produce general answers
🔹 5.6 Applications
Homework
Quick facts
General knowledge
🔹 5.7 Case Study

🧩 7. Missing Word Prompting
🔹 7.1 Definition
Missing Word Prompting is a technique where part of a sentence is left blank, and the AI is
asked to fill in the missing word(s).
🔹 7.2 Structure
Sentence with blanks:
Example:
“The capital of India is ______.”
🔹 7.3 Examples
“Water freezes at ______ degrees Celsius.”
“The largest planet is ______.”
🔹 7.4 Features
Interactive
Tests knowledge
Used in learning
🔹 7.5 Advantages
Enhances memory
Engages users
Good for quizzes
🔹 7.6 Disadvantages

Not suitable for deep learning

❓ 8. Preceding Question Prompting
🔹 8.1 Definition
Preceding Question Prompting involves asking a series of related questions before the main
question to guide the AI toward a better answer.
🔹 8.2 Structure
Step-by-step questioning:
What is energy?
What are types of energy?
Explain renewable energy
🔹 8.3 Example
Prompt:
What is photosynthesis?
What are its steps?
Explain with diagram
🔹 8.4 Features
Logical flow
Step-by-step reasoning
Detailed output
🔹 8.5 Advantages
Produces detailed answers
Improves clarity
Helps in complex topics
🔹 8.6 Disadvantages
Time-consuming
Requires careful design
🔹 8.7 Applications
Research
Complex problem solving
Teaching
🔹 8.8 Case Study
Instead of: “Explain climate change”
